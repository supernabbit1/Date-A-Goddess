No. Date a Goddess offers strictly professional, platonic and non-sexual companionship. Sexual or intimate services are never offered.

The $250/hour rate covers professional companionship and discreet professional security. Dining, events, transportation and other date expenses are paid separately by the client.

How does security work?

Professional security accompanies every booking. They remain discreet but present at all times. The policy is mandatory and cannot be waived.

How is my privacy protected?

Both parties review a mutual non-disclosure agreement before the booking is finalized. Photography and recording also require explicit consent.

What is the cancellation policy?

A 24-hour cancellation policy applies. Full details are provided with the booking terms before finalization.

Do you travel?

Yes. Bookings are available across Canada. Out-of-province dates add a $500 travel fee per day, and multi-day experiences are available by request.
